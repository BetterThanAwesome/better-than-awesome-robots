The robot market is getting visually spectacular very quickly. That creates a problem for buyers. It is easy to start with the machine instead of the work.

A better starting point is the job.

What happens repeatedly? What is difficult to staff? What exposes people to unnecessary risk? What requires long walking routes, repeated lifting, repetitive cleaning, continuous inspection or predictable internal delivery?

Once the work is defined, the robot becomes much easier to evaluate.
