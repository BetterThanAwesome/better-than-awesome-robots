## What it is

The PUDU MP2000 is a mobile material handling platform designed around pallet movement and internal logistics.

## Why it matters

A robot does not need a face to be useful. In warehouses and production environments, moving material from one predictable place to another can be a far more immediate automation opportunity than deploying a humanoid.
