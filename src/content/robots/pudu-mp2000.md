## What it is

The PUDU MP2000 is an autonomous pallet handling robot designed for industrial and logistics workflows. The current RobotsUSA listing describes a maximum load of 2,000 kg, 3D LiDAR-SLAM plus VSLAM navigation, and integrations including APIs, elevators, automatic doors, and IoT devices.

## Why it matters

A robot does not need a face to be useful. In warehouses and production environments, moving material from one predictable place to another can be a far more immediate automation opportunity than deploying a humanoid.

## Questions for buyers

What routes repeat every day? How many employee hours go into internal movement? How often do those movements create delays? What safety requirements apply around forklifts, people and loading areas?

This product is currently presented by RobotsUSA as quote-only, so configuration, regional compatibility, deployment planning, and project pricing should be confirmed with the seller.
