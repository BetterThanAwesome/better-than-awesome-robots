## What it is

RobotsUSA currently sells a PUDU hotel starter bundle centered on autonomous cleaning and delivery. The listing presents the CC1 cleaning platform as part of the package.

## Why Better Than Awesome is watching it

Bundles are where robotics starts becoming an operating model instead of a gadget purchase. A hotel may have cleaning, room delivery, guest service and back-of-house movement happening at the same time. The real question is whether several narrow robots can remove enough repetitive work to justify the combined deployment.

## Buyer questions

Which workflows are being automated first? Are elevators and doors compatible? Who dispatches and monitors the fleet? What happens after hours? How many staff hours are actually displaced, redirected or improved?

Bundle contents, pricing and delivery timing can change, so confirm the current package with the seller.
