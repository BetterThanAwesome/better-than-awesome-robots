## What it is

The KEENON S100 is currently listed by RobotsUSA as a heavy-load courier robot for internal logistics and material movement.

## Why it matters

Internal transport is a strong automation target because the work is repetitive, measurable and usually tied to known routes. That makes it easier to compare robot cost against labor time, delays, injuries, throughput and utilization.

## Buyer questions

How many trips happen per shift? What loads are actually moved? Are routes shared with forklifts or pedestrians? What doors, elevators or systems need integration? What happens when the robot cannot complete a route?

Confirm payload, navigation, warranty, integration requirements and current seller pricing before purchase.
