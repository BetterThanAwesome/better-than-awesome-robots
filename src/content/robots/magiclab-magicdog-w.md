## What it is

The MagicLab MagicDog-W is a wheeled quadruped platform marketed for research, education and development. RobotsUSA currently lists it at $12,500.

## Why Better Than Awesome is watching it

Quadrupeds are interesting because mobility is the product. A useful evaluation should focus on the environments it can reach, the sensors and compute available to developers, payload, runtime, secondary development support and what the wheels actually add compared with a conventional legged platform.

## Buyer questions

Do you need stairs, rough terrain, speed on flat surfaces, or all three? What development stack is supported? Which sensors and compute modules are included? Is this a research platform, demonstration platform or operational machine for your use case?

Confirm the exact included configuration and development capabilities with the seller.
