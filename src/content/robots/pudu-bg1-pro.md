## What it is

The PUDU BG1 Pro is a large autonomous scrubber-dryer robot for commercial and industrial floor care. The current RobotsUSA listing describes one-pass sweeping and scrubbing, 3D LiDAR plus VSLAM positioning, AI spot cleaning, and optional workstation support.

## Why Better Than Awesome is watching it

Cleaning is one of the easiest robot categories to understand economically. The questions are practical: how much floor area needs to be covered, how often, what labor is available, how much supervision is required, and whether the machine can operate safely around people.

## Buyer questions

How much floor area needs routine cleaning? What surfaces and obstacles are involved? Will the robot share space with the public? Who handles refilling, drainage, consumables, maintenance, and recovery when something goes wrong?

Pricing and availability can change, so confirm the current configuration with the regional seller before purchasing.
