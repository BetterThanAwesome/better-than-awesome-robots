## What it is

RobotsUSA currently lists the Deyee Polyenergy-King AI Physiotherapy Robot at $23,400.

## Why Better Than Awesome is watching it

Healthcare robotics can create meaningful value, but the buying threshold is higher than ordinary service robotics. Clinical evidence, regulatory status, training, patient safety, infection control and integration into professional care workflows all matter.

## Buyer questions

What clinical use is supported? What evidence exists for outcomes? What regulatory status applies in your jurisdiction? Who is qualified to operate it? How is patient data handled? How is the device cleaned and maintained?

Do not treat marketplace availability as proof of clinical effectiveness or regulatory clearance. Verify independently before deployment.
