## What it is

DJI positions the Matrice 4T for emergency response, public safety and forestry. Its wildfire workflow uses visual and thermal sensing for initial risk evaluation, hotspot identification and post-incident documentation.

## Why Better Than Awesome is watching it

This is a strong example of robotics that can increase responder awareness without asking a person to enter the most dangerous part of the scene first.
