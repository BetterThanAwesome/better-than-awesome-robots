The CleaniBot S55 Pro is an autonomous commercial floor-cleaning robot listed by RobotsUSA at $16,995. The seller describes sweeping, scrubbing, vacuuming, mopping, self-cleaning and disinfection functions.

## Why it matters

Cleaning is unusually measurable. Buyers can compare area covered, cleaning frequency, staffing hours, consumables, downtime and supervision against an existing process.

## Buyer questions

What square footage needs cleaning each day? What floor types are involved? How much human setup or recovery is required? Who handles water, detergent, consumables and maintenance? Can the unit safely share space with customers and staff?

Treat coverage, safety and performance figures as seller specifications until confirmed for your facility.
