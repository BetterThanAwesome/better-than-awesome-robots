## What it is

The Fourier GR-3 is a care-oriented full-size humanoid. RobotsUSA currently lists it at $119,995 and positions it around companionship, education, clinical environments and public service.

## Why Better Than Awesome is watching it

Companion robots should be judged differently from industrial robots. The important questions are trust, interaction quality, privacy, accessibility, emotional expectations, reliability and whether the robot meaningfully supports people rather than merely attracting attention.

## Buyer questions

Who will interact with it? What personal data is processed or stored? What supervision is required? What happens when a user becomes emotionally attached to the system? What clinical or care claims are actually validated?

Confirm current configuration, intended use and support with the seller before purchase.
