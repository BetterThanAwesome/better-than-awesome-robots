## What it is

The FarmDroid FD20 is a solar-powered autonomous field robot for precision seeding and weeding. FarmDroid says it uses RTK GPS with 8 mm precision, supports up to 12 active rows and a 3.5 meter working width, and can cover up to 6 hectares per day depending on conditions.

## Why Better Than Awesome is watching it

This is the kind of robot that makes shopping by job useful. A farmer does not need a generic list of robots. They need to know what crops the machine supports, how many hectares it can cover, how it handles weeds, what implements are available, how service works locally and what the economics look like.
