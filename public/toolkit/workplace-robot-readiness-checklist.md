# Workplace Robot Readiness Checklist

Use this before selecting a specific robot.

1. Define one repeated task clearly.
2. Document the current process.
3. Measure current labor time and cost.
4. Identify safety risks.
5. Map the physical route and operating environment.
6. Identify charging or battery requirements.
7. Review network and data requirements.
8. Identify required integrations.
9. Define human oversight.
10. Create a failure and recovery procedure.
11. Check insurance and legal requirements.
12. Confirm service, parts and warranty access.
13. Plan staff communication and training.
14. Run a limited pilot before expanding.
15. Measure results against the original task.
