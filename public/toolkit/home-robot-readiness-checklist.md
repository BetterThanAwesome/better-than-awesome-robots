# Home Robot Readiness Checklist

Before buying a home robot, ask what problem it is solving.

Check floor types, stairs, thresholds, pets, children, charging location, WiFi coverage, camera and microphone privacy, cloud service dependence, replacement parts, subscription costs and what happens if the manufacturer stops supporting the product.

If the robot can move heavy objects, operate doors, access exterior areas or interact physically with people, plan supervision and safety boundaries before deployment.
